    How this works

    This page refreshes its information once a day. It collects headlines from trade news feeds, the latest recall records from NHTSA and end-of-day stock prices, then updates this page. Nothing is written by hand and nothing is copied from articles. You see a headline, its source and a link.

    Lawsuit and deal headlines report what outlets published. They are claims and reports, not findings.
